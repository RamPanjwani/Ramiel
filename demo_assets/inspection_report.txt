1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE
The scheduled quadrennial overhaul inspection was conducted on separation train 4.
All static vessels, control valves, rotating pumps, and associated instrumentation
were nondestructively tested (NDT) via ultrasonic thickness gauging and acoustic emission.

2. EQUIPMENT OBSERVATIONS
- Valve V-101 (High Pressure Separator Inlet):
  Nominal design pressure: 15.0 bar. Observed operating pressure: 12.2 bar.
  Seat leakage: Zero. Valve travel: Nominal 100%. Status: ACCEPTABLE.

- Valve V-102 (Acid Gas Bypass Control Valve):
  Nominal design pressure: 15.0 bar. Observed operating pressure: 17.2 bar (EXCEEDS LIMIT by 14.7%).
  Severe acoustic emission indicating localized cavitation and seal erosion.
  Recommendation: IMMEDIATE REPLACEMENT / REFURBISHMENT REQUIRED before startup.

- Pump P-201 (Condensate Booster Pump):
  Operating vibration: 4.1 mm/s RMS (Design limit: 4.5 mm/s RMS).
  Bearing temperature: 68 deg C. Status: ACCEPTABLE (Schedule monitoring in 30 days).

- Vessel TK-301 (Flash Gas Accumulator):
  Wall thickness: 18.2 mm (Minimum allowable thickness: 15.0 mm). Corrosion rate: 0.1 mm/yr.
  Status: FIT FOR CONTINUED SERVICE.

- Line 6"-HC-1004 (Hydrocarbon Transfer Header):
  NDT weld scans clean. No hydrogen-induced cracking (HIC) detected.

3. RECOMMENDATIONS & SIGN-OFF
1. Halt commissioning of Unit 4 train until Valve V-102 is replaced with an upgraded Inconel trim valve.
2. Re-calculate hydraulic pressure drop and safety relief flow across V-102 bypass loop.
3. Submit Executive Approval Note for emergency spare procurement ($42,000 budget authorization).
